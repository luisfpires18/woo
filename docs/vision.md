# Vision and principles

Updated: 2026-10-05. Status: design documentation; no game implementation yet.

## Confirmed

Original persistent browser strategy inspired by Travian and BFME. Kingdom defence and village-territory conquest replace independent alliances. Player identity is master blacksmith, with full control of their own settlement resources, recruitment and military actions. Named kingdom rulers are separate characters, likely unique heroes.

Kingdoms need distinct, viable rosters with strengths and weaknesses. Craftsmanship, ordinary forging and later runeforging should affect military choices. Start medieval and evolve gradually.

## Accepted direction

Village-centred districts, useful kingdom duties, differentiated camps, regiment identity, pre-battle stances, fortress modules and capture objectives. These require concrete rules and first-version scoping.

## Proposed experience goals

Useful support/defensive participation, recovery after defeat, understandable combat, manageable attendance, mobile usability and no purchased combat power. A 20–40 minute daily target was suggested but not confirmed.

## Design risks

Forging becoming a minor stat button beside settlement administration; dominant faction choices; empty fronts in a small alpha; endless elimination; fantasy or naval scope entering the first version prematurely. Validate these with playable tests.
