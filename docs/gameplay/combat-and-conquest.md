# Combat and conquest

Updated: 2026-10-05. Status: design documentation; no game implementation yet.

## Confirmed

No fake attacks or manually synchronised waves. Players defend their kingdom. Capture a village to transfer its district and expand the conquering kingdom. Each unit has strengths and weaknesses; mounted horse units can beat appropriate foot troops but struggle against pikemen.

## Accepted direction

Regiment identity, unit counters, pre-battle stances, fortress modules and capture objectives. Combat timing must be balanced, but no schedule is selected.

## Proposed resolution

Declared visible campaigns, advance troop commitments and automatic server resolution. Composition, terrain and stance affect outcomes. Avoid requiring live micro. Limit simultaneous campaigns. These operational details need approval.

## Counter model proposal

Separate combat role, movement domain, armour class and later rune. Use readable conditional counters plus stats, rather than universal cavalry superiority or a huge multiplier matrix. Details and formulas are open.

## Open

Who declares shared campaigns; contributions/withdrawal deadlines; travel; fog/scouting; damage model; casualty/wounded split; siege victory; retreats; night truce; offline defence; simultaneous actions; infrastructure damage; capture governor; elimination/recovery.

## Acceptance scenarios to define

Prepared pikes versus cavalry; mixed armies; offline defender; identical battle inputs reproducible; no duplicated casualties on retries; capture changes owner once; interrupted workers recover a due battle. Exact outcomes require chosen mechanics.
