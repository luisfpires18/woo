# Seasons and victory

Updated: 2026-10-05. Status: design documentation; no game implementation yet.

## Confirmed direction

Seasonal kingdom competition. Moraphys is intended as the final villain faction, with hostile outposts. Exact first medieval season ending is undefined.

## Tentative future finale

Defeat Moraphys and Chaos weapon wielders; imprison recovered weapons or use them at severe recoil. Defeating all rival kingdoms was suggested as part of victory but explicitly not fixed. Not all Chaos weapons are assigned to Moraphys. Containment, possession, recoil and corruption need separate rules.

## Proposals

A 10–12 week foundation/discovery/Chaos/Order arc; territorial/objective scoring or ancient-site stabilisation; seasonal reset of military/territory/relic possession with persistent cosmetic history. None is confirmed. A competitive Moraphys finale may avoid demanding every rival's total destruction.

## Open

First-version victory without runes; season length; end deadline; tie-breaking; capital defeat; recovery; NPC escalation; reset rules; persistent talent/skill; late joining; rune emergence during a season versus release update.

The medieval first version supersedes any assumption that a Chaos/Order finale is its launch requirement.
