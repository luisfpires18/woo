# Sylvara: standing unit sprite prompts
Updated: 2026-10-05. Status: visual test prompts, not canon, approved assets or a confirmed first-release roster.

Source: owner's uploaded `sylvara.md`, read in full on 2026-10-05. Names and weapon/mount assignments are retained from that draft. Small clothing, pose and creature-anatomy choices below are appearance proposals. No unit removed or merged.

## How to test
Copy ONE complete prompt below per generation. Start with Warden for a basic comparison across kingdoms. Generate Arkazia Bastion first; if you approve its rendering, attach that actual image as STYLE ONLY when testing Veridor Legionary and Sylvara Warden. Then reuse the same accepted style reference for other units. Keep character/faction identity from the prompt, not from the style image. Without an actual shared reference, matching text helps but cannot guarantee consistency.

These are static art tests for a battalion board. No animation or sprite-sheet work. Mounted entries are one rider-and-mount game unit in one neutral pose; the horse/creature is not omitted. Future mounts and unusual weapons remain draft tests, not medieval-v1 commitments. Beastmaster's companion is intentionally a separate future asset to keep the single-character test.

Inspect all three basic outputs side by side at the same displayed size: camera, proportions, outlines, shading, light, role silhouette and alpha edges. View against white and charcoal; a drawn checkerboard is not alpha transparency. No image is automatically approved or uploaded.

## 01. Warden
Test classification: foot.

```text
Create exactly ONE full-body standing game-unit sprite for Weapons of Order. Hand-drawn 2D cel-shaded art with clean confident dark outlines, simple readable shapes and three controlled value bands per material. Use the same stylised human proportion system for every unit: approximately six heads tall, slightly enlarged readable hands/feet, natural limb anatomy and no exaggerated chibi head. Keep detail broad enough to read when reduced to a small battalion icon. No photorealism, 3D rendering, painterly texture or pixel art.

Use a consistent orthographic-like three-quarter view, facing toward the right, camera around chest height with only slight visibility of the top planes. Full body, head, feet, hands and every weapon completely inside a square canvas, with generous transparent padding. Neutral standing ready pose, calm expression, no attack, running, jumping or action. Light comes from upper left; use the same outline weight, shade-step language and material treatment as the supplied approved STYLE reference if present. Do not copy that reference's identity, clothing, colours, weapons or pose.

UNIT: Sylvara Warden.
FACTION PALETTE: deep forest green cloth, muted gold accents, warm brown leather and carved heartwood, minimal neutral steel.
UNIT DESIGN: Human woodland line infantry. Wear crafted bark/heartwood protective plates over leather and forest-green cloth, with small muted gold accents. Carry one spear in the right hand and one plain leaf-shaped wooden shield in the left. Stand calmly with the whole spear visible. Human ears and anatomy; no elf traits or living magical vines.

Exactly one human fighter, with no mount, companion, extra person or duplicate figure.

True transparent alpha background. Request PNG output on a square canvas, ideally 1024 x 1024; no background colour, painted checkerboard, scenery, floor, pedestal, vignette or cast shadow patch. No text, labels, numbers, UI, logo or watermark. One output image only, no sprite sheet, turnaround, multiple poses, contact sheet or collage. No glow, runes, aura or magical effects unless explicitly required in the unit description.

Keep feet/hooves/paws on one implied level ground line near the bottom, with no drawn ground. Leave enough padding for the full equipment silhouette. Preserve the shared camera/proportions/style rather than redesigning the rendering for this kingdom.
```
